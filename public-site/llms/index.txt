1a:T2092,# SlainteTech

> Healthcare workflow, patient safety, hospital billing recovery, and EU NIS2 cybersecurity software built on HL7 FHIR R4. SlainteTech separates fast clinician documentation from tested, deterministic Python safety gates (:8081) and tamper-proof FHIR R4 audit logging (:8082), connecting alongside Epic, Cerner, or MEDITECH in 15 minutes via SMART-on-FHIR v2 or running stand-alone in private cloud and offline environments.

Full technical specification & FAQ reference: https://slainte-tech.com/llms-full.txt
Sitemap: https://slainte-tech.com/sitemap.xml
Contact & 30-Day Pilot Booking: hello@slainte-tech.com

- [Platform & Safety Architecture](https://slainte-tech.com/platform/): Four-layer architecture separating HTMX frontline screens (:8000), 16 modules, FastMCP Python safety gates (:8081), and SlainteFHIR R4 (:8082).
- [Security, GDPR Art. 9 & NIS2 Compliance](https://slainte-tech.com/security-compliance/): Built-in EU NIS2 Article 21 safeguards, GDPR Article 9 redaction, DCB0129/0160 engineering, OASIS SARIF v2.1.0 outputs, and offline execution.

- [Investors & Strategic Rationale](https://slainte-tech.com/investors/): Defensibility thesis built on deterministic clinical safety, 15-minute SMART-on-FHIR v2 hospital entry, and 8 verticals on one codebase.

- [Book a 30-Day Pilot](https://slainte-tech.com/contact/): Start a zero-disruption read-only hospital audit, clinic walkthrough, or investor briefing.

## Eight Healthcare Verticals
- [For Emergency Departments (SlainteED)](https://slainte-tech.com/verticals/emergency-departments/): Manchester Triage Cat 1-5 queue, RCP NEWS2 sepsis tracking, ambulance pre-alert handover, and 3-tier prescribing checks.
- [For General Practitioners & Community Nursing (SlainteGP)](https://slainte-tech.com/verticals/general-practice/): Fast house-call and surgery notes, chronic disease recalls, kidney-checked prescriptions, and monthly HSE GMS claims export.
- [For Psychiatrists & Mental Health (SlaintePsych)](https://slainte-tech.com/verticals/psychiatry/): Calm sensory workspace, PHQ-9 and GAD-7 longitudinal tracking, psychotropic drug checks, and locked sensitive notes.
- [For Dental Surgeons & DSOs (SlainteDent)](https://slainte-tech.com/verticals/dentistry/): Interactive 32-tooth FDI odontogram, 6-sextant BPE gum charting, bleeding/allergy banners, and safe post-op prescribing.
- [For Clinical & Community Pharmacies (SlaintePharm)](https://slainte-tech.com/verticals/pharmacy/): Risk-sorted dispensing queue flagging allergy, high-alert drug pairs, and renal holds plus clean monthly HSE PCRS exports.
- [For Ambulance & Paramedic Services (SlainteEMS)](https://slainte-tech.com/verticals/ambulance-ems/): Large glove-friendly 44px tablet ePCR, offline vital scoring, and 1-tap hospital emergency bay pre-alert.
- [For Elite Sports Clubs & Federations (SlainteSport)](https://slainte-tech.com/verticals/sports-medicine/): Squad medical readiness, SCAT6 concussion return-to-play clearance logs, TUE checks, and athlete privacy locks.
- [For Astronaut & Aerospace Medicine (SlainteAstro)](https://slainte-tech.com/verticals/astronaut-health/): 100% air-gapped onboard medical station with offline clinical safety rules, zero external dependency, and capsule vitals tracking.
